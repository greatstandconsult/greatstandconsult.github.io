WHO WE ARE

More than tutorials. We help students navigate the journey.

Great Stand Educational Consult is a modern online educational platform established in November 2024 by A.O. Joshua and Joseph Olawale.

We combine academic preparation with educational and admission guidance, helping students understand what they need to learn, what they need to do and what to expect as they transition into higher education.

Discover our story ↗
OUR PURPOSE

Helping students prepare today for the opportunities of tomorrow.

At Great Stand, we believe success goes beyond simply passing an examination. Students need quality learning, accurate information, proper preparation and guidance to make informed academic decisions.

MISSION

To provide accessible, quality and practical educational support that helps students perform better academically, make informed admission decisions and confidently transition into higher education.

VISION

To become a trusted digital educational platform known for academic excellence, reliable admission guidance and student success.
